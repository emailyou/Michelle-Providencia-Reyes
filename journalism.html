Campus Journalism

From Grade 9 to the RSPC

My journey as a campus journalist began in Grade 9 as a member of Ang Banaag, our school publication. Through this experience, I developed my writing, communication, and storytelling skills.

My dedication to journalism led me to become an RSPC qualifier, and I also received a Special Award for Campus Journalism. These experiences strengthened my passion for expressing ideas through words and communicating meaningful stories to others.

  • Member of Ang Banaag
  • RSPC Qualifier
