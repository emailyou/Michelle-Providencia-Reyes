Community Service

Tzu Chi medical outreach, Ipil

One of my meaningful experiences was participating in a medical outreach program organized by the Tzu Chi Foundation in Ipil, Zamboanga Sibugay, at the Provincial Capitol. Being part of this community activity allowed me to experience the importance of compassion, volunteerism, and service to others.

It reminded me that achievements are not measured only by awards and academic recognition, but also by our willingness to contribute to the community and help make a positive difference in people's lives.
