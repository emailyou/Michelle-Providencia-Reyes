Leadership and Organizational Involvement

Serving the school community

Youth for Environment in Schools Organization

During senior high school, I served as the Federated Vice President of the Youth for Environment in Schools Organization (YES-O). Through this role, I participated in environmental initiatives and student activities that encouraged responsibility and environmental awareness. My involvement was also recognized through the Luntiang Medalya Award.

PenKasining, the Filipino Club

I also served as the Vice President of PenKasining, our Filipino Club, where I developed my leadership, teamwork, and appreciation for the Filipino language and literature. These experiences taught me the value of service, cooperation, and contributing positively to the school community.
