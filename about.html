About Me

I am Michelle Providencia Reyes, a passionate, creative, and goal-oriented student who values education, leadership, and self-expression. I completed my senior high school education at Kabasalan National High School and am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, major in Financial Management (BSBA-FM), at AMMC.

Beyond academics, I enjoy writing poetry, playing Scrabble, and solving puzzles such as Sudoku and Nonograms. My passion for creative writing inspired me to establish a Facebook page dedicated to my original poetry, which has grown to approximately 40,000 followers. I also value community involvement and leadership, as these experiences have taught me the importance of responsibility, teamwork, and service to others.

As I continue my college journey, I aspire to expand my knowledge, strengthen my abilities, and prepare for a successful future in financial management. I believe that every experience is an opportunity to learn, grow, and become a better version of myself. Through my creativity, dedication, and perseverance, I hope to inspire others and make a meaningful contribution to the community.
